Now recall our definitions of the $a_{r}$ and $b_{r}$ as Künneth components relative to a fixed base

$\alpha_{j}\in H^{1}( M )$. They imply the formulae

\[ c_{r}= a_{r}+ \sum_{j}\alpha_{j}b_{r}^{j}+ f_{r}\omega , \]

where $c_{r}= c_{r}( V )$ and $a_{r}, f_{r}, b_{r}^{j}$ are classes on $N$ identified with their pullback to $M \times N$. We also

\[ c_{r}= a_{r}+ \xi_{r}+ f_{r}\omega \]

\[ \xi_{r}= \sum \alpha_{j}b_{r}^{j}. \]

As a consequence note that the $\xi_{r}$ are nilpotent of order three: $\xi_{r}\xi_{s}\xi_{t}= 0$, and that $\xi_{r}\omega = 0$,

while $\xi_{r}\xi_{s}$ is a multiple of $\omega$. We therefore set

\[ \xi_{r}^{2}= 2 \Lambda_{r r}\omega \]

and

\[ \xi_{r}\xi_{s}= \Lambda_{r s}\omega , \quad r \neq s . \]

In terms of the skew form $\mu_{i j}$ given by the intersection pairing in $H^{1}( M )$, that is

\[ \alpha_{i}\alpha_{j}= \mu_{i j}\omega , \]

\[ \Lambda_{r r}= - \frac{1}{2}\sum \mu_{i j}b_{r}^{i}b_{r}^{j}, \quad \Lambda_{r s}= - \sum \mu_{i j}b_{r}^{i}b_{s}^{j}\]

so that these are integral non-degenerate forms in the $b_{r}$. They are pertinent for our purposes

because of the following easily proved result.
