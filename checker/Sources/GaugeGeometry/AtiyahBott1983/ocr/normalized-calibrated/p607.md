In the good range $k > n / p$ one can also define nonlinear generalizations of the spaces $L_{k}^{p}$.

Thus one can define the space $L_{k}^{p}( M , N )$ of maps $f : M \rightarrow N$ where $N$ is another $C^{\infty}$ manifold.

More generally one can define the $L_{k}^{p}$-sections of a $C^{\infty}$ fibre bundle over $M$ with $N$ as fibre. These

spaces are dense in the space of continuous sections. In particular on taking $N$ to be a Lie group

and using (14.5) the $L_{k}^{p}$ automorphisms of a vector bundle (or a unitary bundle) form Lie groups.

We come now to the special case that interests us, namely $n = \operatorname{dim}M = 2$. For a complex $C^{\infty}$

vector bundle $E$ over $M$ with hermitian metric we then have for $k \geqslant 2$ the real Lie group of

unitary automorphisms of class $H^{k}= L_{k}^{2}$, which we shall denote by $\mathscr{G}^{k}$. Its 'complexification'

complex Lie group $\left(\mathscr{G}^{c}\right)^{k}$ also acts smoothly on $\mathscr{A}^{k - 1}$.

For $k > 2$ the space $\mathscr{A}^{k - 1}$ consists of continuous connections. However, the most natural space

for our purposes is in fact $\mathscr{A}^{1}$, so the reader should remember that this includes discontinuous

connections. A little more care will be necessary in various places but there is no fundamental
