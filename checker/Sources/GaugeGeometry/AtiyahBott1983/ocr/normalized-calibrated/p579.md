The triviality of the fibration (9.2) when $( n , k ) = 1$ is essentially equivalent to the existence of

a (topological) universal or Poincaré bundle over $M \times N$ as we shall now explain. By definition

a universal bundle is a holomorphic vector bundle $V$ over $M \times N$ so that for all $n \in N$ the restriction

$V_{n}$ of $V$ to $M \times \{n \}$ is in the isomorphism class represented by the point $n$. The projective bundle

$P ( V )$ exists naturally. To see this we recall that we have an obvious holomorphic bundle $W$ over

[?line conf=0.94: $M \times \mathscr{C}_{\text{ss}}$ and $\mathscr{G}^{\mathrm{c}}$ acts holomorphically on $W$ with the constant scalars $C^{*}$ acting trivially on the]

[?line conf=0.88: base and as scalars in the fibre of $W$. Thus $\overline{\mathscr{G}}\overline{\mathrm{c}}= \mathscr{G}^{\mathrm{c}}/ C^{*}$ acts freely on $P ( W )$ and the quotient]

gives $P ( V )$ over $M \times N$. A universal vector bundle is therefore a 'lift' back from this natural

projective bundle over $M \times N$. If $V$ is holomorphic on each $M_{n}$ but only continuous in $n$ we refer to

it as a topological universal bundle.

Quite generally there is an obstruction to the extension of such a lift called the 'Brauer class'.

It arises from the sequence

\[ 1 \rightarrow C^{*}\rightarrow G L ( n ) \rightarrow P G L ( n ) \rightarrow 1 \]

and lies in $H^{2}\left(M \times N , \mathcal{O}^{*}\right)$ where $\mathcal{O}^{*}$ is the sheaf of multiplicative holomorphic functions. Taking

the coboundary of the exponential sequence

\[ 0 \longrightarrow Z \xrightarrow{\mathrm{e}^{2 \pi \mathrm{i}}}\mathcal{O}\longrightarrow \mathcal{O}^{*}\longrightarrow 0 \]

leads to the topological Brauer class, which is an $n$-torsion class in $H^{3}( M \times N , Z )$. Explicitly in

terms of transition matrices $p_{i j}$ for a $P G L ( n )$-bundle we lift these locally to $g_{i j}$ in $G L ( n )$ and define

the scalar $\lambda_{i j k}$ by the formula

\[ g_{i j}g_{j k}= \lambda_{i j k}g_{i k}. \]

[?line conf=0.73: This is a 2-cocycle for $\mathcal{O}^{*}$ and taking determinants shows that $\lambda_{i j k}^{n}$ is a coboundary.]

In our case since $M$ and $N$ (by (9.9)) are torsion-free it follows that the topological Brauer
