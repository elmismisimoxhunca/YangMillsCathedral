To understand the geometric significance of (8.1) we recall first, as explained in §5, that a

isomorphism

[?line conf=0.79: \[ \Omega^{1}( \mathfrak{u}( n ) ) \cong \Omega^{0,1}( \mathfrak{g}\mathfrak{l}( n , C ) ) \]]

bundle, whereas $\mathscr{A}$ and hence the isomorphism $\mathscr{A}\rightarrow \mathscr{C}$ depend on such a choice. The connection

The proof of Narasimhan \& Seshadri (1965) is essentially of (8.2). It is easy to prove the

[?line conf=0.88: inclusion $\mathcal{N}_{\mathrm{s}}\subset \mathscr{C}_{\mathrm{s}}$ and infinitesimal arguments show that the map of quotients (which are]

manifolds) is a local diffeomorphism. The hardest part of the proof is the surjectivity and this

requires compactifying both sides and a consequent induction on $n$. The real explanation for

(8.2) is probably to be found in the moment map ideas indicated at the end of §10 (see also

remark 2 above).
