Using the holomorphic structure defined by $\mathrm{d}_{A}^{\prime \prime}$ we shall continue our analysis of Yang-Mills

in the two-dimensional case and discuss the implication of having a connection $A$ that is extremal

for $L ( A )$ and therefore satisfies the equation

(5.2)

\[ \mathrm{d}_{A}* F ( A ) = 0 \]

Now $F ( A ) \in \Omega^{2}( M , \operatorname{ad}( P ) )$ and hence $* F ( A ) \in \Omega^{0}( M ; \operatorname{ad}( P ) )$, and is therefore a section of

$\operatorname{ad}( P )$, on which both $\mathrm{d}_{A}'$ and $\mathrm{d}_{A}^{\prime \prime}= 0$. Thus (5.2) implies that $* F ( A )$ is a holomorphic section of

$\operatorname{ad}( P )$, which is covariant constant, and we claim that we can therefore decompose $\operatorname{ad}( P )$

completely according to the eigenvalues of the endomorphism

\[ \Lambda = \mathrm{iad}* F ( A ) , \]

that is

\[ \Lambda \alpha = \mathrm{i}[ * F ( A ) , \alpha ] , \quad \alpha \in \Omega^{*}( M ; \operatorname{ad}( P ) ) . \]

More precisely, we claim that the eigenvalues of $\Lambda$ on $\operatorname{ad}( P )$ are locally constant, and, as $\Lambda$ is

self-adjoint, there is therefore a natural decomposition

[?line conf=0.62: \[ \operatorname{ad}( P ) \otimes C = \oplus_{\lambda}\operatorname{ad}_{\lambda}( P ) , \quad \lambda \in R , \]]

of $\operatorname{ad}( P ) \otimes C$ into orthogonal sub-bundles where $\Lambda$ reduces to the scalar $\lambda$.

Furthermore, (5.2) now implies that the induced decomposition

\[ \Omega_{C}^{*}( M ; \operatorname{ad}( P ) ) = \oplus_{\lambda}\Omega^{*}\left(M ; \operatorname{ad}_{\lambda}( P )\right) \]

is stable under $\mathrm{d}_{A}$. The local constancy of the eigenvalues of $\Lambda$ follows by considering the

trace functions $\operatorname{tr}\Lambda^{n}, n = 1,2 , \ldots$. We have

[?line conf=0.81: \[ \mathrm{d}\operatorname{tr}\Lambda^{n}= \operatorname{tr}\mathrm{d}_{A}\Lambda^{n}= 0 . \]]

Actually our main concern will be with the decomposition

[?line conf=0.81: \[ \operatorname{ad}( P ) \otimes C \cong \operatorname{ad}^{-}( P ) \oplus \operatorname{ad}^{*}( P ) \oplus \operatorname{ad}^{+}( P ) \]]

corresponding to negative, zero, and positive eigenvalues of $\Lambda$. Note also that the Reimannian

metric on $\operatorname{ad}( P )$ induces on complexification holomorphic dualities

(5.3)

\[ \operatorname{ad}^{*}( P ) \cong \operatorname{ad}^{*}( P )^{*}, \quad \operatorname{ad}^{-}( P )^{*}\cong \operatorname{ad}^{+}( P ) . \]

With these matters understood we now have the following formulae for the index and nullity.

Proposition 5.4. Let $A$ be an extremal connection for $P$. Then

\[ \begin{aligned}& \operatorname{nullity}( A ) = 2 \operatorname{dim}_{C}H^{1}\left(M ; \operatorname{ad}^{\#}( P )\right) , \\ & \operatorname{index}( A ) = 2 \operatorname{dim}_{C}H^{1}\left(M ; \operatorname{ad}^{-}( P )\right) , \end{aligned}\]

where $H^{i}$ denotes the cohomology of the sheaf of holomorphic sections of the bundle indicated.

Proof. From the discussion in §4 we have to compute the nullity and index of the quadratic

form

[?line conf=0.86: \[ \hat{Q}( \eta ) = \left(\Delta_{A}\eta + \hat{F}\eta , \eta\right) , \quad \eta \in \operatorname{Kerd}_{A}^{*}, \]]

in $\Omega^{1}( M ; \operatorname{ad}( P ) )$, where as before we have written $\hat{F}$ for the transformation

\[ \hat{F}: \eta \rightarrow * [ * F ( A ) , \eta ] . \]
