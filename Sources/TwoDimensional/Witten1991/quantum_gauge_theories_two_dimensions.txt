Abstract. Two dimensional quantum Yang-Mills theory is studied from three
points of view: (i) by standard physical methods; (ii) by relating it to the large k
limit of three dimensional Chern-Simons theory and two dimensional conformal
field theory; (iii) by relating its weak coupling limit to the theory of Reidemeister-
Ray-Singer torsion. The results obtained from the three points of view agree and
give formulas for the volumes of the moduli spaces of representations of
fundamental groups of two dimensional surfaces.

exceptional sets of positive codimension will not contribute, and will frequently be
discarded. (Among other things, it is for this reason that we need not be concerned
about singularities of ~g.)

length of this section comes from the attempt to be self-contained in explaining the
combinatorial torsion and from the fact that we work out several cases in detail.
Further background about combinatorial torsion can be found in [-11-13].
