Next we shall prove

Proposition 10.4. The canonical parabolic reduction is functorial with respect to group homomorphisms.

Thus let $\phi : G^{\mathrm{c}}\rightarrow H^{\mathrm{c}}$ be a homomorphism, $\xi$ a principal $G^{\mathrm{c}}$-bundle, and $\eta = \phi ( \xi )$ the associated

$H^{\mathrm{c}}$-bundle. Then we have a homomorphism of Lie-algebra bundles

\[ \phi : \operatorname{ad}( \xi ) \rightarrow \operatorname{ad}( \eta ) . \]

Since $G^{\mathrm{c}}$ is reductive the homomorphism of Lie algebras induced by $\phi$ has $G^{\mathrm{c}}$-invariant comple-

ments to the kernel and image. Hence putting $E = \operatorname{ad}( \xi ) , F = \operatorname{ad}( \eta )$ we can decompose

$\phi : E \rightarrow F$ into split exact sequences

\[ 0 \rightarrow K \rightarrow E \rightarrow I \rightarrow 0 , \quad 0 \rightarrow I \rightarrow F \rightarrow J \rightarrow 0 . \]

Now for any direct sum $A \oplus B$ of vector bundles it is easy to see, using 10.3, that the parts of the

canonical filtration with slope $\geqslant 0$ are additive: $( A \oplus B )_{0}= A_{0}\oplus B_{0}$. Applying this to our

situation we see that

\[ E_{0}\cong K_{0}\oplus I_{0}, \quad F_{0}\cong I_{0}\oplus J_{0}\]

so that $\phi$ sends $E_{0}$ into $F_{0}$. This proves that the canonical parabolic reduction of $\eta$ is induced

by that of $\xi$.
