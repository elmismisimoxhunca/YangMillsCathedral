11. Comparison with Harder-Narasimhan approach

As mentioned in the Introduction the Poincaré polynomials of the moduli spaces of stable

bundles have been computed by number-theory methods in Harder \& Narasimhan (1975) and

Desale \& Ramanan (1975). In this section we shall compare those methods with ours.

We begin with an example by spelling out in detail our results for the simplest interesting case,

namely for $n = 2$ and $k = 1$. Our basic theorem 7.14 becomes

(11.1)

[?line conf=0.82: \[ \mathscr{G}P_{t}\left(\mathscr{C}_{\mathrm{s}}\right) + \sum_{r = 0}^{\infty}t^{2 ( 2 r + g )}\mathscr{G}P_{t}\left(\mathscr{C}_{r}\right) = \mathscr{G}P_{t}( \mathscr{C}) \]]

where $\mathscr{G}P_{t}$ stands for $\mathscr{G}$-equivariant Poincaré series and $\mathscr{C}_{r}$ is the stratum corresponding to

[?line conf=0.88: unstable bundles of type $( r + 1 , - r )$ (i.e. of the form (11.10)). As shown in § 9 (see (9.3) and (9.7)),]

for the stable bundles we have

\[ \mathscr{G}P_{t}\left(\mathscr{C}_{\mathrm{s}}\right) = \frac{P_{t}( N ( 2,1 ) )}{1 - t^{2}}= \frac{( 1 + t )^{2 g}P_{t}\left(N_{0}( 2,1 )\right)}{1 - t^{2}}. \]

For the unstable stratum $\mathscr{C}_{r}$ we apply (7.12) to see that

\[ \mathscr{G}P_{t}\left(\mathscr{C}_{r}\right) = \left\{\frac{( 1 + t )^{2 g}}{1 - t^{2}}\right\}^{2}. \]

Finally for the whole space we apply theorem 2.15, which, for $n = 2$, gives

\[ \mathscr{G}P_{t}( \mathscr{C}) = P_{t}( B \mathscr{G}) = \frac{\left\{( 1 + t ) \left(1 + t^{3}\right) \right\}^{2 g}}{\left(1 - t^{2}\right)^{2}\left(1 - t^{4}\right)}\]

Substituting these into (11.1) and cancelling a common factor $( 1 + t )^{2}/ \left(1 - t^{2}\right)$, we get

(11.2)

\[ P_{t}\left(N_{0}( 2,1 )\right) + \frac{( 1 + t )^{2 g}}{1 - t^{2}}\sum_{r = 0}^{\infty}t^{2 ( 2 r + g )}= \frac{\left(1 + t^{3}\right)^{2 g}}{\left(1 - t^{2}\right) \left(1 - t^{4}\right)}. \]

Summing the geometric series we see that this gives the formula

(11.3)

\[ P_{t}\left(N_{0}( 2,1 )\right) = \frac{\left(1 + t^{3}\right)^{2 g}- t^{2 g}( 1 + t )^{2 g}}{\left(1 - t^{2}\right) \left(1 - t^{4}\right)}. \]
