6. Representations of the fundamental group

In the previous section we saw that, over a compact connected Riemann surface $M$, a con-

nection $A$ is extremal for the Yang-Mills functional if and only if $* F ( A )$ is covariant constant

(relative to $A$ ), that is

(6.1)

\[ \mathrm{d}_{A}* F ( A ) = 0 \]

In particular, if our $G$-bundle is topologically trivial, a flat connection, that is with $F = 0$,

necessarily satisfies (6.1) and corresponds to the absolute minimum of the Yang-Mills functional.

Now a flat $G$-connection is locally trivial and globally corresponds to a homomorphism

(6.2)

\[ \pi_{1}( M ) \rightarrow G \]

describing the holonomy. Solutions of (6.1) that are non-zero can be described in a similar

manner by using a suitable central extension of $\pi_{1}( M )$.

We recall that $\pi_{1}( M )$ is a group with $2 g$ generators $A_{1}, B_{1}, \ldots , A_{g}, B_{g}$ satisfying the single

relation

(6.3)

[?line conf=0.93: \[ \prod_{i = 1}^{g}\left[A_{i}, B_{i}\right] = 1 , \]]

where $[ A , B ]$ is the commutator $A B A^{- 1}B^{- 1}$. It follows that, for $g \geqslant 1 , \pi_{1}( M )$ has a universal

central extension

\[ 1 \rightarrow Z \rightarrow \Gamma \rightarrow \pi_{1}( M ) \rightarrow 1 , \]

where $\Gamma$ is generated by $A_{1}, B_{1}, \ldots , A_{g}, B_{g}$ and a central element $J$ satisfying the single relation

(6.4)

\[ \prod_{i = 1}^{g}\left[A_{i}, B_{i}\right] = J . \]

Let $\Gamma_{R}$ denote the group obtained from $\Gamma$ by extending the centre to $R$, so that we have a central

extension:

(6.5)

\[ 1 \rightarrow R \rightarrow \Gamma_{R}\rightarrow \pi_{1}( M ) \rightarrow 1 . \]

On dividing by $Z$ the group $\Gamma_{R}$ clearly becomes a direct product

(6.6)

\[ 1 \rightarrow Z \rightarrow \Gamma_{R}\rightarrow U ( 1 ) \times \pi_{1}( M ) \rightarrow 1 . \]
