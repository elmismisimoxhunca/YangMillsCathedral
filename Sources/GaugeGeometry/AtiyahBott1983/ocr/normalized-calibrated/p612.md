Proposition 15.4 shows that, for an algebraic family of bundles over $M$, the canonical stratifi-

cation is continuous. To prove the continuity of the map (14.10) it remains now to show that we

can always construct 'sufficiently large' algebraic families. More precisely we need to show that

for any $A \in \mathscr{E}$ we can find a smooth transversal $N$ to the $\mathscr{G}^{\mathrm{c}}$-orbit through $A$ that represents

(locally) an algebraic family. This means we have to prove the following lemma.

Lemma 15.5. Let $E_{0}$ be an algebraic vector bundle over the algebraic curve $M$. Then there exists an algebraic

family of bundles $E_{s}$ parametrized by a non-singular variety $S$ such that

(i) $E_{0}\cong E_{s_{0}}$ for some $s_{0}\in S$,

(ii) the infinitesimal deformation map

\[ \phi : T_{s_{0}}( X ) \rightarrow H^{1}\left(M , \operatorname{End}E_{0}\right) \]

is an isomorphism.
