From (8.16) and the convention we have adopted for defining $\phi$ on $n$-vectors (absorbing the

factor $- 2 \pi \mathrm{i}$ ) we then obtain

(8.18)

\[ \Phi ( A ) \geqslant \phi ( \mu + a ) . \]

[?line conf=0.58: But since $a_{1}\geqslant 0 , a_{2}\leqslant 0$ with trace $a_{1}= - \operatorname{trace}a_{2}$ it follows easily that $\mu + a \geqslant \mu$ with respect to]

[?line conf=0.85: the partial ordering defined in (7.7). As will be explained in § 12 this implies, and in fact is]

equivalent to,

\[ \phi ( \mu + a ) \geqslant \phi ( \mu ) \]

for all convex invariant $\phi$. This completes the proof of proposition 8.13 for the two-step case.

As can be seen the essence of the proof is the basic principle that 'curvature decreases in holo-

morphic sub-bundles and increases in quotients' (Griffiths \& Harris 1978, p. 79). The general

case of (8.13) proceeds in the same manner and we simply have to keep track of the notation.

The details are as follows.

We start with a holomorphic bundle $E$ with its canonical filtration of type $\mu$ :

\[ 0 = E_{0}\subset E_{1}\subset \ldots \subset E_{r}= E \]

where the quotients $D_{j}= E_{j}/ E_{j - 1}$ have normalized Chern classes $\mu^{j}$ with

\[ \mu^{1}> \mu^{2}> \ldots > \mu^{r}. \]

The curvature $F ( A )$ can then be expressed in a block form generalizing (8.14). For every $j < k$

we have an element

\[ \eta_{j k}\in \Omega^{0,1}\left(M , \operatorname{Hom}\left(D_{k}, D_{j}\right)\right) \]

so that $\mathrm{d}\eta_{j k}$ appears in the $( j , k )$-block. The $\eta_{j k}$ are the components of the element

\[ \eta_{k}\in \Omega^{0,1}\left(M , \operatorname{Hom}\left(D_{k}, E_{k - 1}\right)\right) \]

related to the exact sequence

\[ 0 \rightarrow E_{k - 1}\rightarrow E_{k}\rightarrow D_{k}\rightarrow 0 . \]

Now define scalar non-negative $m_{j}\times m_{j}$ matrices $a_{j k}$ for $j < k$ by

\[ \operatorname{trace}a_{j k}= \frac{1}{2 \pi \mathrm{i}}\int_{M}\operatorname{trace}\left(\eta_{j k}\wedge \eta_{j k}^{*}\right) \geqslant 0 , \]

and define $a_{k k}$ by

\[ \operatorname{trace}a_{k k}= \frac{1}{2 \pi \mathrm{i}}\int \operatorname{trace}\eta_{k}^{*}\wedge \eta_{k}\leqslant 0 , \]

[?line conf=0.87: so that $\sum_{j \leqslant k}$ trace $a_{j k}= 0$. Then the convexity of $\phi$ leads to the inequality]

\[ \Phi ( A ) \geqslant \phi [ \mu + a ] , \]

where $a$ stands for the vector (or diagonal matrix) whose $j$ th block $a^{j}$ is the scalar (matrix)

\[ a^{j}= \sum_{k \geqslant j}a_{j k}. \]

Equivalently the vector $a$ can be written as a sum

\[ a = \sum b_{k}\]
