We note finally that the universal bundle is not unique and can be altered by tensoring with

any holomorphic line-bundle $L$ on $M \times N$. On $M$ the universal property implies that $L$ must

have degree zero (and must moreover satisfy $L^{n}= 1$ ) but the Chern class on $N$ is arbitrary, as is

the component in $H^{1}( M ) \otimes H^{1}( N )$.
