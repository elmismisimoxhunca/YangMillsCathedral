If we fix a base-point of $\mathscr{F}_{\mu}$ corresponding to a definite $C^{\infty}$ filtration $E_{\mu}$ of $E$, the fibre of (7.9)

over this point is the subspace $\mathscr{B}_{\mu}\subset \mathscr{C}_{\mu}$ of complex structures compatible with the given filtration.

If $\operatorname{Aut}\left(E_{\mu}\right)$ is the group of $C^{\infty}$ automorphisms of $E$ preserving the filtration then $\operatorname{Aut}\left(E_{\mu}\right)$ acts

on $\mathscr{B}_{\mu}, \mathscr{F}_{\mu}$ is the homogeneous space $\operatorname{Aut}( E ) / \operatorname{Aut}\left(E_{\mu}\right)$ and $\mathscr{C}_{\mu}$ can be identified with the associated

bundle. Hence for the purposes of equivariant cohomology the pairs

\[ \left(\operatorname{Aut}( E ) , \mathscr{C}_{\mu}\right) \quad \text{and}\quad \left(\operatorname{Aut}\left(E_{\mu}\right) , \mathscr{B}_{\mu}\right) \]

[?line conf=0.89: are equivalent as explained in § 13.]

Next let us choose splittings of the filtration $E_{\mu}$ so that we get a direct sum decomposition $E_{\mu}^{0}$ of $E$

(7.10)

\[ E = D_{1}\oplus D_{2}\oplus \ldots \oplus D_{r}\]

with

\[ E_{i}= D_{1}\oplus \ldots \oplus D_{i}. \]

and let $\operatorname{Aut}E_{\mu}^{0}, \mathscr{B}_{\mu}^{0}$ be the automorphisms and complex structures (in $\mathscr{B}_{\mu}$ ) compatible with this

decomposition. Then we have

(7.11)

\[ \begin{aligned}\operatorname{Aut}\left(E_{\mu}^{0}\right) & \cong \prod_{l = 1}^{r}\operatorname{Aut}\left(D_{i}\right) \\ \mathscr{B}_{\mu}^{0}& \cong \prod_{l = 1}^{r}\mathscr{C}_{\mathrm{ss}}\left(D_{i}\right) \end{aligned}\]

On the other hand the natural homomorphism

\[ \operatorname{Aut}\left(E_{\mu}\right) \rightarrow \operatorname{Aut}\left(E_{\mu}^{0}\right) \]

is a homotopy equivalence, and the fibration

\[ \mathscr{B}_{\mu}\rightarrow \mathscr{B}_{\mu}^{0}\]

has a vector space as fibre and is compatible with the group actions. It follows that, for purposes

of equivariant cohomology, the pairs

\[ \left(\operatorname{Aut}\left(E_{\mu}\right) , \mathscr{B}_{\mu}\right) \quad \text{and}\quad \left(\operatorname{Aut}\left(E_{\mu}^{0}\right) , \mathscr{B}_{\mu}^{0}\right) \]

are equivalent. Together with (7.11) this finally yields for rational cohomology

Proposition 7.12. The equivariant cohomology of the stratum $\mathscr{C}_{\mu}( E )$ is isomorphic to the tensor

product of the equivariant cohomology of the semi-stable strata for the quotients $D_{i}$.

Here of course the equivariant cohomology is always taken with respect to the automorphism

group of the appropriate bundle.

We also need to look at the equivariant cohomology of the conormal bundle $N_{\mu}$ to $\mathscr{C}_{\mu}$ in $\mathscr{C}$.

By this we mean of course the appropriate relative cohomology or the cohomology of the Thom

[?line conf=0.88: space of $N_{\mu}$. Exactly the same reduction as above shows that we can replace the triple (Aut $( E )$,]

[?line conf=0.29: $\left. \mathscr{C}_{\mu}, N_{\mu}\right)$ by the triple (Aut $\left(E_{\mu}^{0}\right) , \mathscr{B}_{\mu}^{0}, N_{\mu}^{0}$ ) where $N_{\mu}^{0}$ is the restriction of $N_{\mu}$ to $\mathscr{B}_{\mu}^{0}$. Now from (7.11)]

[?line conf=0.78: we see that $\operatorname{Aut}\left(E_{\mu}^{0}\right)$ contains the $r$-dimensional torus $T^{r}$, which acts trivially on $\mathscr{B}_{\mu}^{0}$. To show that]

our stratification is perfect it remains to show, using (1.9) and (13.4), that the representation

of $T^{r}$ on the fibre of $N_{\mu}$ is primitive. Now at a point of $\mathscr{B}_{\mu}^{0}$ our bundle $E$ is a holomorphic direct

sum of the $D_{i}$ and so the bundle $\operatorname{End}'E$ of endomorphisms preserving the filtration is the direct

[?line conf=0.57: sum of $\operatorname{Hom}\left(D_{i}, D_{j}\right)$ for $i \geqslant j$. Hence]

(7.13)

[?line conf=0.91: \[ \operatorname{End}^{\prime \prime}E \cong \underset{i < j}{\oplus}\operatorname{Hom}\left(D_{i}, D_{j}\right) . \]]
