Some Remarks on the Gribov Ambiguity

I. M. Singer
Department of Mathematics, University of California, Berkeley, California 94720, USA, and
Massachusetts Institute of Technology, Cambridge, Massachusetts 02139, USA



      Abstract. The set of all connections of a principal bundle over the 4-sphere with
      compact nonabelian Lie group under the action of the group of gauge
      transformations is studied. It is shown that no continuous choice of exactly one
      connection on each orbit can be made. Thus the Gribov ambiguity for the
      Coloumb gauge will occur in all other gauges. No gauge fixing is possible.
