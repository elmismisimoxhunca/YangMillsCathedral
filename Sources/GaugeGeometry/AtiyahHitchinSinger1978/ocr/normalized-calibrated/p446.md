We now have the vanishing result and so

[?line conf=0.55: \[ \operatorname{index}\left(\mathrm{d}+ \mathrm{d}^{*}\right) = - h^{0}+ h^{1}- h^{2}= h^{1}\]]

and we may use the index theorem (Atiyah \& Singer 1968) to evaluate the alter-

nating sum. For the Dirac operator

[?line conf=0.94: \[ \mathrm{D}: \Gamma \left(V_{+}\otimes E\right) \rightarrow \Gamma \left(V_{-}\otimes E\right) , \quad \operatorname{index}\mathrm{D}= \operatorname{ch}( E ) \hat{\mathscr{A}}( X ) [ X ] \]]

[?line conf=0.53: where $\operatorname{ch}( E ) =$ Chern character of $E$, and $\hat{\mathscr{A}}( X ) = \widehat{A}$ polynomial in the Pontrjagin]

classes of $X$. In four dimensions $\hat{\mathscr{A}}( X ) = 1 - \frac{1}{24}p_{1}( X )$. If $E = V_{-}\otimes \mathrm{g}$, then

[?line conf=0.63: \[ \begin{aligned}\operatorname{index}\mathrm{D}& = \operatorname{ch}( \mathrm{g}) \operatorname{ch}\left(V_{-}\right) \hat{\mathscr{A}}( X ) [ X ] \\ & = p_{1}( \mathrm{~g}) + \operatorname{dim}G \left(\mathrm{index}\mathrm{D}: \Gamma \left(V_{+}\otimes V_{-}\right) \rightarrow \Gamma \left(V_{-}\otimes V_{-}\right)\right) \\ & = p_{1}( \mathrm{~g}) - \frac{1}{2}\operatorname{dim}G ( \chi - \tau ) , \end{aligned}\]]

and so this is the dimension of $H^{1}( \mathrm{~g})$, which is going to be the tangent space at $\omega$

to the space of moduli. Recall that $p_{1}( \mathrm{~g}) \geqslant 0$ for a self-dual connection.

2. We have completed the infinitesimal computation. Next we have to integrate

[?line conf=0.82: these infinitesimal deformations and show that every element in $H^{1}( g )$ is defined]

[?line conf=0.93: by a 1-parameter family. For this we follow closely the argument of Kuranishi]

(1965). From (6.2), a self-dual connection is given by a solution of the non-linear

equation

\[ p_{-}\left(\mathrm{D}_{1}\tau + \frac{1}{2}[ \tau , \tau ]\right) = 0 \quad \tau \in A^{1}( \mathrm{~g}) . \]

For ease of notation, let us write this as

\[ \mathrm{d}\tau + \{\tau , \tau \}= 0 \]

[?line conf=0.79: where $d$ is the differential in the complex]

\[ A^{0}( \mathrm{~g}) \xrightarrow{\mathrm{d}}A^{1}( \mathrm{~g}) \xrightarrow{\mathrm{d}}A_{-}^{2}( \mathrm{~g}) . \]

Consider the set $\Phi$ of self-dual connections

\[ \Phi = \left\{\tau \in A^{1}( \mathfrak{g}) : \mathrm{d}\tau + \{\tau , \tau \}= 0 \quad \text{and}\quad \mathrm{d}^{*}\tau = 0 \right\}. \]

[?line conf=0.89: We shall show that a neighbourhood of the origin of $\Phi$ is a local space of modulı,]

[?line conf=0.93: but first we show that $\Phi$ is an $h^{1}$ dimensional manifold with tangent space $H^{1}( \mathrm{~g})$]

at the origin.

Take the Green operator $G$ of the Laplacian $\mathrm{dd}^{*}+ \mathrm{d}^{*}\mathrm{~d}= \Delta$ of the complex. Then

\[ G \Delta = 1 - H , \]

where $H$ is the orthogonal projection onto the harmonic subspace. Define a map

$F : A^{1}( \mathrm{~g}) \rightarrow A^{1}( \mathrm{~g})$ by

[?line conf=0.82: \[ F'( \tau ) = \tau + G \mathrm{~d}^{*}\{\tau , \tau \}; \]]

then

\[ \begin{aligned}\mathrm{d}F ( \tau ) & = \mathrm{d}\tau + \mathrm{d}G \mathrm{~d}^{*}\{\tau , \tau \}\\ & = \mathrm{d}\tau + G \mathrm{~d}\mathrm{~d}^{*}\{\tau , \tau \}\\ & = \mathrm{d}\tau + \{\tau , \tau \}\quad \text{since}h^{2}= 0 , \end{aligned}\]
