4. Self-dual spaces and complex manifolds

As an application of proposition 3.1, we shall prove the following:

Theorem 4.1. Let $X$ be an oriented 4-manifold. Then a conformal structure on $X$

defines in a natural way an almost complex structure on $P \left(V_{-}\right)$, which is integrable

iff $W_{-}= 0$, i.e. iff $X$ is self-dual. (Note that the spin representations are well defined

projective representations of $S O ( 4 )$, so we need not assume that $X$ is a spin manifold.)
